## Why Ticker?

A free, open-source, on-device **alternative to RescueTime, Timing, Rize, and
ActivityWatch**.

- 🔒 **Private by design** — everything stays on your Mac. No servers, no account, no telemetry.
- 🖥️ **Truly native** — SwiftUI + Swift Charts; a menu-bar companion and a full dashboard.
- 🎯 **Real focus** — continuous-focus streaks, not scattered productive seconds.
- 🧘 **Built-in wellness** — configurable move & screen breaks with on-screen countdowns.
- 🧠 **Smart** — knows *idle at your desk* (reviewable) from *away* (not counted).
